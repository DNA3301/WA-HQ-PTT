FAQ

Common questions

How do I know HQ mode is active?

The WA-HQ-PTT recording status and custom cancel/trash control must appear. If the normal WhatsApp recorder appears instead, restart or repair the helper before testing quality.

Are recordings uploaded to another server?

No project-owned audio server is used. Capture and FFmpeg conversion are local; the finished PTT is handed to WhatsApp for normal delivery.

Why is WA-JS pinned to a nightly build?

The 2026-09-24 nightly fixed the iPhone compatibility problem in the tested media path. The exact bundle is pinned by hash so a future nightly cannot silently replace it.

Is this official WhatsApp software?

No. WA-HQ-PTT is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

In italiano

Vocali WhatsApp Desktop ovattati o compressi?

WA-HQ-PTT v2.0.5 usa un percorso locale HQ OGG/Opus a 48 kHz mono e 128 kbps di default. La compatibilità iPhone è stata corretta senza abbassare la qualità che funzionava correttamente su Android.

Durante la registrazione devono comparire lo stato WA-HQ-PTT e il pulsante cestino/annulla: se non compaiono, non stai usando il percorso HQ.
